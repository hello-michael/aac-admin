    How it works
    01 · Every hour

    The bridge reads every open order in Shopify. Paid orders wait out a 6-hour hold, so buyers can change their minds, then go to Artelo to be printed and framed.

    02 · Holds

    Big orders, territory addresses, buyer emails and anything unusual stop for you. Release them here once you have checked. A new change holds the order again.

    03 · Safety nets

    A failed run pings your phone. If the bridge goes quiet for 2.5 hours, Supabase pings you too. Your Mac keeps an hourly copy of every order.
